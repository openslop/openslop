# Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use OpenSlop, including people with disabilities.

## Goal

We aim for [WCAG 2.2 AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). That's a goal, not a claim we've passed an audit. The basics come first: keyboard access, visible focus, readable text, good contrast, labels, alt text, and respecting reduced motion.

## Found a problem?

Open an issue with "Accessibility" in the title. Tell us what you were trying to do, what got in the way, how to reproduce it (including any keys you pressed), and your browser, OS, and assistive tech. You don't need to name a WCAG rule or suggest a fix. Leave out passwords, private projects, and medical info.

## Fixing one?

Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). In the PR, say what you checked (keyboard, focus, zoom, screen reader) and what you couldn't. Lint catches some issues, but not whether the app actually works with a keyboard or screen reader.
